# Research Evidence Schema — R1

## Required record
- research_id
- claim
- domain
- source_type
- source_title
- source_author
- source_date
- source_locator
- original_language
- primary_or_secondary
- extracted_evidence
- translation_notes if applicable
- contradiction_or_disagreement
- confidence
- engineering_implication
- design_implication
- experiment_candidate
- affected_projects
- reviewed_date

## Confidence guidance
High confidence requires either strong primary evidence or multiple high-quality independent sources.

## Multilingual rule
For Gaudí research, preserve the original-language wording for terms whose meaning may be flattened in translation. Record both literal and interpretive translations when useful.

## Contradiction rule
Disagreement among reputable sources is itself a research result. Do not collapse disputed interpretation into a single fact.

## Promotion
A research record does not become a production decision. It may only:
- update canonical knowledge,
- create a hypothesis,
- create an experiment,
- change the priority of an existing experiment.
