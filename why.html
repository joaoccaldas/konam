WHY KONA.M

Triathlon is bigger
than the result sheet.

Training plans and race results matter. They are not the whole sport.

Triathlon also lives in machines, places, people, history, rituals, tiny technical decisions, ridiculous dreams and the feeling that a road can mean something before you ever ride it.

Kona.m exists to make that larger world explorable.

WHY KONA

A symbolic center.
Not the whole map.

Kona is one of triathlon's most recognizable reference points: a place where machines, heat, wind, history, ambition and race-week culture collide.

That makes it a useful center of gravity for the product. Start from one place with a dense amount of meaning, then extend outward to other races, cities, athletes, machines and worlds.

Kona.m uses that symbolism editorially. It does not imply official affiliation, endorsement or ownership of the event, place, brands or athletes it references.
