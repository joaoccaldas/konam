# Competitive position and business model

## Market competitors with different jobs
- [99 Spokes](https://99spokes.com/en/data) — multi-brand bike/spec/pricing data. Licensed feed may accelerate breadth, subject to commercial terms.
- [Geometry Geeks](https://www.geometrygeeks.bike/) and [Bike Insights](https://bikeinsights.com/) — geometry and fit comparison, not a reason to invent a new geometry standard.
- [BikePedia](https://www.bikepedia.com/Year.aspx) — historical specifications; cannot take its name.
- [VeloBase](https://www.velobase.com/) — historical components; evidence community model exists.
- [Bicycle Blue Book](https://www.bicyclebluebook.com/value-guide/) and [Pinkbike BuySell](https://www.pinkbike.com/buysell/list) — valuation and liquid marketplaces; don't launch a generic classifieds clone.
- [ProBikeGarage](https://www.probikegarage.com/), [BikeCare](https://www.bikecare.app/) and [Velo Garage](https://velogarage.app/) — maintenance/training mileage tracking; needs more than another reminder app to win.
- [Shimano Technical Documents](https://si.shimano.com/en/) and [SRAM](https://www.sram.com/en/service) — authoritative service documentation; link to, don't overwrite.
- [Bike Index](https://bikeindex.org/documentation/api_v3) — potential future stolen bike trust adapter subject to approval and privacy.
- [Park Tool](https://www.parktool.com/blog/repair-help) — quality service education, not a service content corpus to copy.

## Differentiation hypothesis
One sourced product/component identity traverses exact assembly 3D, cross-brand comparison, documented historical evolution, supported compatibility, maintenance, garage and later moderated community and offers. Asset reuse/production efficiency is a second potential B2B advantage. **This hypothesis is unvalidated until user tasks and paying partner interviews.**

## Business ladder
(1) Paid B2B proof-of-value digital twins/product education and partner embeds; (2) approved affiliate product links; (3) optional premium multi-bike Garage; (4) manufacturer/retailer documentation/embedding/license subscriptions; (5) eventually mediated listings and transaction fees. Safety specs remain free, commerce never changes evidence ranking.

## Risks and disconfirming evidence
- Missing licensed manufacturer assets/source rights;
- customers choose specialist tools and don't value the convergence;
- maintenance/CAD content costs and brand/rights restrictions;
- consumer/social marketplace chicken-and-egg, fraud, dispute and moderation costs;
- an attractive 3D feature generates sessions but not completed real tasks;
- inability to measure reliable compatible/incompatible judgments.

## Validation interviews
15–20 triathletes, 5 mechanics, 3 retailers using identical tasks in existing tools and Rotaverk. Capture time-to-correct-answer, failures, comprehension of certainty, source trust, recurring use and willingness to pay. At least 8 B2B conversations before pricing conclusions.
