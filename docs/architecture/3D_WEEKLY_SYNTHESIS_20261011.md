# Unified 3D R&D | Weekly decision review, 4–11 October 2026

**Decision: retain the federated architecture.** The week proved better scene identity, release staging, selected visual/performance methods and a few local contracts. It **did not prove a cross-repository shared renderer**, final-room equivalence, measured Studio-Kona streaming gains or competition-approved Gaudí imagery. No production promotion, merge, dependency upgrade or asset replacement follows from this review.

**Evidence classes:** **measured** = an instrumented result on the named scene; **observed** = inspected pixels or a passing contract/CI on a pinned ref; **candidate** = code/manifest exists without an A/B or downstream adoption; **blocked** = preflight prevented the relevant measurement. All confidence values are evidence-conditioned judgments, not statistical probabilities.

## Rejections, retirement and uncertainties

**Rejected as current solutions:** wholesale KONA [#123](https://github.com/joaoccaldas/konam/pull/123) merge (divergent, nonmergeable draft); old NOR/Breitling public URLs as evidence of the *final* rooms; three full canonical bike clones inside older NOR (~1.53m triangles/317 calls against budget); identical `.blend` file hashes as proof of semantic reproducibility; Gaudí's old ~5 m roof beneath a required 4.5 m upper level; and reducing mesh complexity until the scene becomes visually poorer. These are bounded rejection decisions, not universal bans on the underlying methods.

**Retire duplicated work by designation, not deletion:** designate `canyonmuseum` a heritage/provenance source rather than a parallel KONA release owner (Oct 2 copy-and-revert history demonstrates the risk). Keep `rawdog3d` archived as declared in [its README](https://github.com/joaoccaldas/rawdog3d/blob/main/README.md), with `rawdogging` as its stated successor; no changes were seen on their default branches this week. Treat [BlocksCreateWorld](https://github.com/joaoccaldas/blockscreateworld) as a 2025 Canvas2D/historical gameplay reference, **not** a current 3D runtime. Preserve the superseded NOR, Breitling and Beast review rooms as provenance/visual baselines but retire them from canonical navigation only *after* exact-room certification. Retire duplicate room-local loaders/renderers only after the second tested consumer, never by blanket refactor.

**Still uncertain:** final Beast/NOR Winter/Breitling room readiness; scene-matched Bellagio visual/interaction equivalence; universal AssetSpec and renderer portability; quality of Breitling HERO materials and macro glass; KTX2 variants, ClusterLOD/HLOD, WebGPU/Three.js dev path, splat/mesh hybrid, Blender importer upgrades, and CFD promises. Seven [daily frontier deltas](https://github.com/joaoccaldas/konam/tree/research/3d-core-r1-20261004/research/evidence) update *experiment conditions*; they contain **no controlled cross-project performance A/B**. No new platform dependency is justified.

## What made scenes visually better, and what caused repeated failures?

**Measured/observed winners.** NOR #107 removed an occluding service wall and made the Fjord/Kona features spatially readable while merging and instancing repeated meshes. Its call and triangle reductions are measured on the older candidate. Gaudí's [visual refinement](https://github.com/joaoccaldas/ai/blob/codex/gaudi-visual-quality-20261005/projects/gaudi-nexus/docs/VISUAL_REFINEMENT_2026-10-09.md) replaced overwhelming puddles and oversaturated fins with quieter materials, deterministic figures and grounded placement. Crucially, **camera placement and occlusion-ray preflight** turned a black arrival and opaque market frame into visibly legible arrival/market footage at [remote run 37919606164](https://github.com/joaoccaldas/ai/actions/runs/37919606164). But F3 composition, low-detail municipal context and competition hero quality **still fail review**. Gaudí [#100](https://github.com/joaoccaldas/ai/pull/100) proved municipal-first sightlines for 9/9 sample rays; [#101](https://github.com/joaoccaldas/ai/pull/101) found zero non-municipal blockers for 54 sampled shadow rays. Neither is an annual daylight, certified engineering or photorealism claim. The [two-level coordinated candidate](https://github.com/joaoccaldas/ai/blob/codex/gaudi-visual-quality-20261005/projects/gaudi-nexus/docs/ARCHITECTURE_COORDINATION_V2_2026-10-09.md) corrects a physical floor/roof conflict and measures 1,644.296 m² *slab surface*, not certified GFA.

**Repeated failure roots.** (1) **Wrong authority/scene:** old room URLs or proxy Gaudí context masquerading as final source. (2) **Wrong build boundary:** generated output differs from committed output, or JSON is present in repo but absent in staged deployment. (3) **Wrong evidence level:** CI job green is treated as visual approval; renderer errors are inferred when the job never reached a browser. (4) **Wrong camera truth:** opaque geometry obstructs the interesting subject; scene-authored rays/pixel checks were absent. (5) **Wrong reproducibility target:** Blender serialization bytes confused with scene identity. (6) **Wrong architectural assumptions:** floor and roof, stall widths, source datum/CRS, material slots and contact anchors not reconciled before rendering. Build a *single explicit gate* for source SHA, semantic ID, staging, camera rays, frame, visual score and rights before praising the output.
