Privacy & data in this beta

Updated 2 October 2026. Kona.m is an independent beta at joaoccaldas.github.io/konam. Public email sign-in is unavailable until production email delivery and account-support arrangements are ready. You can use the app without an account.

On your device

Your avatar, optional name, answers, race goals, equipment, discoveries and preferences are saved in this browser. We do not request medical records or run an analytics tracker. Export device data creates a local file; Delete device data removes app-owned browser records, including local authentication state. It does not delete a cloud backup or account.

Optional cloud records

Existing signed-in beta users can manually back up their game state to Supabase. A backup can include an uploaded avatar image, goals and equipment. Backups are linked to the signed-in account, restricted to that account, and remain until replaced or deleted. Delete cloud backup in Progress removes the active saved state; it does not remove the Auth account or immediately purge provider operational backups/logs. Account erasure and retention procedures have not yet been cleared for public account launch.

Public services

GitHub Pages serves this site. Google Fonts loads typography, Wikimedia and YouTube can serve images, and requested weather/news features contact public providers through the app or Supabase. These providers receive request information such as IP address. Videos and external links open at their provider; nothing autoplays. Loading an image is not equivalent to being fully offline or making no network requests.

Scope and support

This is an interim behavior notice, not legal clearance. A verified operator/contact, processing basis, retention schedule and applicable international-transfer/rights process must be published before public account registration is enabled. Do not put personal details in public repository issues. Source and non-personal bug reports: project repository.
