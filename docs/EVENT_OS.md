# Event OS Strategy

Goal: make the Kona product more useful before, during and after race week than a conventional tracker/site, while remaining able to ingest official live timing later.

## Benchmark

IRONMAN Tracker already performs the narrow live race-day job well:
- live splits and pace estimates;
- athlete placement;
- course maps;
- multi-athlete following;
- notifications;
- event updates.

We should complement and eventually integrate this layer rather than rebuilding licensed timing first.

## Differentiating layers

### Before race
- race-week timeline;
- official schedule with freshness metadata;
- trip/logistics;
- Race Setup;
- course exploration;
- sponsor/expo discovery;
- commerce/referrals.

### During race week
- what-to-do-now;
- venue context;
- saved places;
- athlete/spectator modes;
- current official updates;
- product and history experiences.

### Race day
- official timing adapter if licensed;
- spectator next-best-viewing guidance;
- context around course/weather/places;
- service/sponsor locations;
- official emergency/safety links.

### After race
- results;
- replay/story;
- setup used;
- media;
- memories;
- commemorative commerce;
- next-race discovery.

## Organizer value

The product must demonstrate:
- stronger athlete utility;
- stronger spectator utility;
- richer sponsor inventory;
- measurable referral/product intent;
- reusable event templates;
- faster content operations;
- one data model across web/PWA/MCP/embed.
