# Affiliate Revenue Priority — Verified Programs

Updated: 2026-09-30

This document distinguishes:
- **published commission mechanics**: official programme publicly states a commission formula/rate;
- **programme available**: applications are currently accepted;
- **approved**: Caldas Studio has actually been accepted.

Only the last state means affiliate tracking may be enabled.

## Tier A — highest-confidence product monetization

### Zwift hardware/accessories
Official programme: Impact.
Published economics: 5% of basket value excluding tax on most Zwift Shop hardware/accessories.
Markets: US, UK, EU.
Membership/subscription commission: currently none.

Product priority:
- Zwift Ride with KICKR CORE 2
- Zwift Ride Smart Frame
- KICKR CORE 2 with Cog/Click
- Cog/Click upgrade
- accessories

Why:
High average basket, direct fit with planned Zwift Lab, clear affiliate terms, measurable product funnel.

### Nike EU
Official programme.
Published economics: up to 11% on valid sales.
Cookie: 30 days.

Product priority:
- Alphafly 3
- Vaporfly 4
- current Nike racing shoes that pass asset/provenance gates

Why:
Strong commission ceiling, direct official product feed, shoe category already entering the platform.

### Specialized
Official programme through Rakuten.
Published economics: 3%-12% on bikes/equipment.
Cookie: 30 days.

Product priority:
- Shiv
- TT helmets
- future Specialized equipment

Why:
High-value products, broad equipment range, clear published commission band.

### Canyon
Official affiliate programme.
Published economics: 2% base on bikes; higher opportunities may exist.
Cookie: 30 days.

Product priority:
- Speedmax CFR
- Speedmax CF SLX
- Canyon equipment/accessories where eligible

Why:
Existing museum already drives deep product intent; direct path from experience to purchase.

### Viator
Official affiliate partner programme.
Published economics: 8% on completed experience bookings within a 30-day attribution window.

Product priority:
- Kona activities
- island tours
- recovery/day-trip experiences
- future race-destination experiences

Why:
Very low implementation friction and directly monetizes Event OS / trip-planner intent.

## Tier B — valuable, but commission not publicly fixed

### Booking.com
Official affiliate programme via approved networks.
Public programme confirms commission on qualified bookings, but public page does not state one universal fixed percentage.

Use for:
- Kona accommodation
- rental cars
- attractions where relevant

Treat as revenue-capable after approval, but do not hard-code an expected commission percentage.

### Garmin
Official affiliate/creator programmes exist.
Public pages confirm commission earning but do not publish a universal public rate for every market/program.

Use for:
- Edge 1050
- wearables
- sensors

Wait for actual programme terms before revenue modelling.

## Tier C — do not prioritize for affiliate revenue now

### Wahoo
Official affiliate programme exists, but the EU page currently states that new applications are not being accepted.

Use:
- ordinary official product links;
- partner/brand outreach;
- Zwift affiliate path where KICKR hardware is sold through Zwift.

Do not design revenue forecasts assuming a new Wahoo affiliate account.

## Revenue certainty rule

```
PROGRAM EXISTS
      ↓
PUBLISHED COMMISSION
      ↓
APPLICATION
      ↓
APPROVAL
      ↓
TRACKING ENABLED
      ↓
QUALIFYING SALE
      ↓
COMMISSION
```

No stage may be skipped in product copy or analytics.

## Product-selection rule

When two equally useful products could be developed next, prefer the one that:
1. has high athlete relevance;
2. has reliable source/asset evidence;
3. fills a reusable platform category;
4. has an active official affiliate programme with published economics;
5. has meaningful basket value.

Commercial opportunity never overrides accuracy, user value or editorial integrity.
