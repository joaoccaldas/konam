ABOUT KONA.M

A triathlon world
you can explore.

Kona.m is an independent experiment in making triathlon feel less like a stack of dashboards and more like a place.

It brings together identity, bikes and equipment, race places, history, stories, collections, 3D scenes, playful progression and practical training context. You can enter through one simple screen and keep discovering deeper layers as curiosity takes over.

DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Simple first.
Deeper when invited.

The first visit should not require understanding the architecture. You can simply enter, create yourself and touch things.

Depth appears progressively: equipment becomes inspectable, places become worlds, stories become rooms, collections become memory, and experiments become tools.

The aim is not to make triathlon more complicated. It is to make the complexity that already exists easier to explore.

INDEPENDENCE + CONTEXT

Built around the sport.
Not pretending to own it.

INDEPENDENT

No affiliation implied

Kona.m is an independent fan, research and product experiment. Referenced races, brands and athletes remain the property of their respective owners.

EVIDENCE

Facts and reconstructions are separated

Published facts, photographed observations, inferred geometry and generated artwork should be identified rather than blended together.

PRIVACY

Local-first by default

No public account is required to start. Profile and progression are designed to remain local unless the visitor explicitly enables a supported sync path.

Kona.m is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by World Triathlon Corporation, IRONMAN, Canyon Bicycles GmbH or any referenced athlete or brand. See Privacy and Credits.
