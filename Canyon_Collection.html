Research & remaining gaps ↗

The bike, on its own

Every exhibit shows the machine alone — no avatar, no guessed rider. Aero figures describe the bike system with stated assumptions; fit, comfort and rider drag need real measurements on a real person.

Physics with provenance

Force, power and time follow stated equations. Imported yaw curves are tied to a setup. Flow trails are illustrative; pressure maps and manufacturer-specific gains require validated measurements or CFD.
