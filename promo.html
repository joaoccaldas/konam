06

FAQ-ish.

reasonable questions, mostly
Q1

Is it a game?

Partly. It borrows identity, progression, discovery and collecting from games, then mixes them with training, equipment, history and places.

Q2

Is it finished?

Enough to exist. Not enough to stop changing. The Intern has decided this is a legitimate production philosophy.

Q3

Where does AI fit?

Generation, agents, analysis, knowledge and future world behavior. It is a capability layer, not a decorative chatbot.

Q4

Why Kona?

Because Kona is a place, a race-week ecosystem and a powerful symbol inside long-course triathlon. It became the anchor for the world.

Q5

Can I help?

Yes. Useful feedback, source material, introductions, broken-button reports and defensible “what if…” ideas are welcome.

GitHub Issues↗
Q6

Do the brands endorse this?

No. Kona.m is independent and unofficial. Referenced trademarks and product names belong to their respective owners.
