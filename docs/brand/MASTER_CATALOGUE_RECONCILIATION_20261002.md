## Correct before treating catalogue as canonical

### Unsupported precision

The catalogue contains exact claims that are not repository-certified launch facts, including examples such as:
- “39 3D + 58 2D + 24 Next100” as a closed Founding 141 production split;
- “117 pages / zero missing image placeholders” as product readiness evidence;
- exact first-paint budget under 250KB without defining transfer/compression/measurement boundary;
- room certification language such as “100% evidence” where runtime/content production is not fully complete;
- exact CdA, rebound-force, athlete wattage, heart-rate, fueling and split telemetry unless each value has a source record and rights/representation review;
- future prices such as $14.99/month or $50k–$150k/year without a validated pricing study/business decision.

These may remain hypotheses or concept examples, but must be labelled as such.

### Partnership language

“Founding Partner”, athlete sanctums and brand pavilions must not imply an active commercial relationship without an agreement. Canyon Museum remains an independent collection and existing disclaimer/provenance rules remain authoritative.

### Cultural / rights risk

Film, athlete, historic footage, archival objects and named-event experiences may involve trademark, copyright, likeness/publicity rights, source licensing and cultural sensitivity. Concept status does not remove those requirements.

### Visual-system mismatch

The catalogue's volcanic-black / amber-horizon treatment is a concept treatment, not a replacement for the canonical launch palette. Canonical launch styling remains sand/lava/ocean/sunrise semantic roles, Instrument Serif + Manrope, artifact-specific shape grammar and optional immersive dark stages.

Golden Five consistency does not mean identical border radii, glass blur or black backgrounds.

### Landing architecture

The catalogue describes current Landing as immediately initializing high-poly WebGL. Current launch architecture intentionally keeps consumer entry lightweight and defers immersive 3D until user intent. Do not regress this.

### Home architecture

The canonical shell has five destinations: Home · Discover · Garage · Plan · Me. A four-button quick rail can exist contextually, but it must not become a second navigation authority.

### Artifact badges

Do not show verbose engineering/audit labels such as CERTIFIED IN-ENGINE PRODUCTION AUDIT · 100% EVIDENCE as primary consumer chrome. Use concise consumer states, with detailed provenance available on demand.
